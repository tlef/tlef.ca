I have the pleasure of being a guest on the latest episode of Reid Hoffman's *Masters of Scale* podcast, which was released today.  

The episode, titled *"The Big Pivot"*, is an interview with Stewart Butterfield, where they discuss the history of Slack, its origins as a failed web game, and the fascinating fact that this is the second time a successful product has risen from the ashes of one of Stewart's failed web games.  

As a developer on one of those failed web games and an early Slack engineer, I was invited to share my involvement in this story.  

You can listen to the podcast here: [Masters of Scale: The Big Pivot](https://mastersofscale.com/stewart-butterfield-the-big-pivot/)
